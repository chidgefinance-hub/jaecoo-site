How Our Service Works (Regulated & Transparent)

  • All customers undergo identity verification and a credit check
  • Vehicles are provided under a private lease agreement
  • Rent-to-own includes an option to purchase at an agreed future price
  • This service is not a loan, finance product, or hire purchase
  • Ownership transfers only after all contractual obligations are met

Compliance & Regulatory Standards

  • Australian Consumer Law (ACL)
  • Victorian Motor Vehicle & Consumer Regulations
  • Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) – handling of personal & credit information
  • Responsible screening and affordability assessment
  • Clear disclosure of pricing, deposits, and obligations

Insurance Requirements

  • All vehicles are comprehensively insured in the owner’s name
  • Insurance includes theft, accident, fire, and third-party damage
  • Excess is payable by the customer in the event of a claim
  • Customers must complete an insurance acknowledgement form prior to handover
  • Unauthorised drivers void coverage and may terminate the agreement

Required Documentation (Mandatory)

  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Valid Australian or international driver licence
  • Proof of income or business activity
  • Credit consent & disclosure form
  • Signed lease or rent-to-own contract
  • Insurance acknowledgement
  • Vehicle condition report
  • Direct debit authority
